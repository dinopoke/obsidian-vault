Ask yourself, what do you want to get out of the jam? Do you want to experiment and try something new? Or hone your skills like it’s your bread and butter?

The ingredients of a good jam are usually Design, Art, Code and Audio but you can find The most important thing is to find people with a shared purpose.

Prep Your Tools. Decide which engine you want to use. Make sure you are all on the same version! Here’s a great list of tools and resources: https://github.com/kobitoko/Game-Jam-Tools-Resources

Back Up Back Up Back Up!
Always have a backup of your work! The best way to do this is to have Version Control. If you don’t know what this is, ask your programmer. If they don’t know, maybe it’s time to find another programmer...

Idea Time

Don’t take too long coming up with an idea but don’t rush into one either. Don’t worry if it only fits the theme loosely as long as your team is excited about it!

Scope Down - mvp
That cool idea you have? Scope it down. Scope it down again. What is the bare minimum you need to make your idea work? That’s probably what you’ll have enough time to make.
