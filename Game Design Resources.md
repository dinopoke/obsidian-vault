I’m not sure if you’ve covered Dan Cook’s Skill Chain Diagrams but they are pretty interesting:
http://www.gamasutra.com/view/feature/129948/the_chemistry_of_game_design.php?page=1
There are also Game Design Logs:
http://www.lostgarden.com/2011/05/game-design-logs.html

I know this recommendation is a book but I’m a big fan of Challenges for Game Designers by Brenda Romero & Ian Schreiber. It is more of an exercise book for paper prototyping than a tome of knowledge. 
https://www.romerogames.ie/shop/challenges-for-game-designers-by-brenda-romero-ian-schreiber

Shreiber also has more material in a set of two online courses that can be followed in your own time:
https://gamedesignconcepts.wordpress.com/2009/09/09/i-just-found-this-blog-what-do-i-do/
https://gamebalanceconcepts.wordpress.com/

Tiny Game Design Tool is a lightweight GDD in booklet form.

Articy Draft for Narrative Design.
https://www.nevigo.com/en/articydraft/overview/

Josh Sawyer has more on other dialogue tools: https://jesawyer.tumblr.com/post/157933744346/3rd-party-dialogue-tools

Raph Koster’s presentation on Gritty Systems Design for Retention: 
https://www.raphkoster.com/games/presentations/gritty-systems-design-for-retention/

Ask A Game Dev is invaluable: http://askagamedev.tumblr.com
Robert Yang’s posts about level design and everything else: https://www.blog.radiator.debacle.us/

Bobby Ross’ Level Design Documents: http://bobbyross.com/tutorials

Game Feel 101:
Juice It Or Lose It  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Fy0aCDmgnxg
The Art of Screenshake https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AJdEqssNZ-U
Also worth reading the articles that are criticisms of these two videos.

Various Board Game Prototype kits. Koster lists what he uses here: https://www.raphkoster.com/2010/10/12/the-fundamentals-of-game-design/

Any of the GDC, GCAP etc Design Talks.

The one is not a game related one but Brian Eno’s Oblique Strategies is useful for creativity in general:
http://stoney.sb.org/eno/oblique.html

I have a lot more but I’m out of time!
