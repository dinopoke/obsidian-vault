Sydney Games Festival!

We are community-run game festival, organised by Sydney's various game-making groups. This is a week-long festival happening between October 13 to October 18. This will be a jam-packed week full of events organised a crossed Sydney by local game development communities in industry, digital and tabletop games.

When? October 13 to October 18
Where? Events across Sydney
