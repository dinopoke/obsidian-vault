A leveling system is essentially a series of goals and rewards for the player
- Rewards should be meaningful
	- Builds anticipation
	- Vary the rewards

- Rewards must arrive in a timely fashion
- Rewards must be earned by player action
	- Gives a sense of scale for the value of each action/objective

## Questions to ask: 

- How many levels do want at maximum?
	- How long should it take to reach maximum level?

- Levels Per Hour - (?)
	- How many LPH should the various activities provide?
